# TW_cloth.md: Paiwan Indigenous Costume

## Basic Information
- Traditional Chinese Title: 台灣排灣族傳統服飾 (Paiwan Costume)
- English Title: Paiwan Indigenous Traditional Costume
- Origin: Southern Taiwan (Pingtung & Taitung Counties)
- Category: Indigenous Textile Heritage & Aristocratic Hierarchy Art
- Associated People: The Paiwan (Payuan) Indigenous Tribe of Taiwan

## Short Introduction
The traditional costume of the Paiwan indigenous people is among the most ornate, dazzling, and symbolic tribal garments in Austronesian culture. Adorned with intricate glass beadwork (Lawa), cross-stitch embroidery, cowrie shells, and sacred hundred-pacer snake motifs, the attire reflects strict traditional social hierarchy, ancestral connections, and nature veneration.

## Deep Cultural Philosophy & Symbolism
1. **The Sacred Hundred-Pacer Snake (Deinagkistrodon acutus / Voru):**
   - Regarded as the spiritual ancestor and guardian deity of the Paiwan nobility. The geometric diamond patterns on the robes represent the snake's scales, granting spiritual protection and noble lineage authority.
2. **Ancient Glass Beads (琉璃珠 - Liuli Beads):**
   - Highly treasured heirlooms passed down generations. Each bead pattern carries a specific spiritual meaning: 'Bead of Nobility', 'Bead of Bravery', 'Bead of Wisdom', and 'Bead of Eternal Love'.
3. **Eagle Feathers & Wild Boar Tusks:**
   - Feathers of the Hodgson's hawk-eagle and wild boar tusks on the headdress are reserved strictly for village chiefs and supreme warriors who demonstrated extraordinary bravery.
4. **Social Stratification (Nobility vs Commoners):**
   - Only aristocrats (Mamazangiljan) historically had the right to wear full bead embroidery, snake patterns, and bird-of-paradise feather headdresses. Today, it stands as a proud symbol of cultural revival.

## Visual & Material Features
- **Color Palette:** Deep black or midnight navy base fabric contrasted with striking scarlet, bright orange, emerald green, and golden-yellow cross-stitch embroidery.
- **Components:** Beaded headbands, ornate sleeveless vest, embroidered robes, chest ornaments (Pansiljan), brass bells, and silver coins jiggling with every step during ritual dances.

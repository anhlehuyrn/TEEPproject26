# VN_food.md: Banh Phu The (Husband and Wife Cake)

## Basic Information
- Vietnamese Title: Bánh Phu Thê (Bánh Xu Xê) Đình Bảng
- English Title: Vietnamese Husband and Wife Cake (Banh Phu The)
- Origin: Dinh Bang Village, Tu Son, Bac Ninh, Vietnam
- Category: Traditional Wedding Delicacy & Symbolic Culinary Art
- Associated Event: Traditional Engagement & Wedding Ceremonies (Le An Hoi, Le Cuoi)

## Short Introduction
Banh Phu The is a legendary Vietnamese sweet delicacy wrapped in green banana leaves. Featuring a translucent, chewy tapioca crust flecked with papaya strands and a rich, golden mung bean filling, this cake is an essential gift in Vietnamese wedding rituals, carrying profound wishes for lifelong fidelity, love, and marital happiness.

## Deep Cultural Philosophy & Symbolism
1. **Yin and Yang Harmony (Am Duong Hoa Hop):** 
   - The outer square box (representing Earth/Yin) and the round cake inside (representing Heaven/Yang) reflect the cosmic harmony necessary for a peaceful, balanced marriage.
2. **The Translucent Jelly Crust (Vo Banh):**
   - Made from refined tapioca/sticky rice flour infused with fragrant gardenia juice (qua danh danh) or pandan, the clear texture represents pure, transparent trust and unbreakable devotion between husband and wife.
3. **The Sweet Golden Mung Bean Core (Nhan Banh):**
   - Mung beans steamed with sugar, grated coconut, lotus seeds, and sesame seeds represent the warmth, sweetness, and fertility of marital life.
4. **The Wrapping Leaves:**
   - Outer layer of green banana leaves (freshness and life) and inner layer of dong leaves tied with red dyed string (steely loyalty and romantic passion).
5. **Historical Legend:**
   - Legend traces the cake to King Ly Anh Tong. When the King went to battle, his Queen personally made this sweet cake and sent it to him on the front lines. Touched by her unwavering devotion, the King named it 'Banh Phu The' (Husband and Wife Cake).

## Visual & Material Features
- **Color:** Translucent golden-amber or jade green exterior, vibrant yellow filling, garnished with white coconut strands and fragrant lotus seeds.
- **Texture:** Chewy, bouncy, crunchy (from papaya shreds), creamy, and delicately sweet.

## Suggested Narration & AI NPC Persona
- Tone: Romantic, joyful, enthusiastic, and culturally profound.
- Key insight: Emphasize that Banh Phu The is never sold or eaten alone—it is always presented in pairs or wrapped in red ribbon to honor the eternal bond of marriage.

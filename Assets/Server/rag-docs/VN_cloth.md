# VN_cloth.md: Vietnamese Ao Tu Than (Four-Panel Dress)

## Basic Information
- Vietnamese Title: Áo Tứ Thân truyền thống Việt Nam
- English Title: Traditional Vietnamese Four-Panel Dress (Ao Tu Than)
- Origin: Northern Vietnam (Kinh Bac region / Red River Delta)
- Category: Traditional Costume & Cultural Heritage
- Historical Era: Worn widely from the 12th century until the mid-20th century

## Short Introduction
The Ao Tu Than (Four-panel dress) is an iconic traditional attire of Northern Vietnamese women. With its flowing silhouette, rustic earthen tones, and graceful silk belt, the costume embodies the timeless virtue, modesty, hard work, and delicate charm of ancient Vietnamese women.

## Deep Cultural Philosophy & Symbolism
1. **The Four Panels (Tu Than):** The four flaps of the dress symbolize the four parents: the wearer's biological parents and her parents-in-law. It represents filial piety (Dao Hieu)—a foundational pillar of Vietnamese ethics.
2. **The Inner Bib (Yem Dao):** The pink or scarlet Yem worn inside represents youthful purity, maidenhood, and discreet femininity.
3. **The Tied Silk Belt (Dai Luu / That Lung):** The contrasting green or yellow silk belt tied gently at the front symbolizes a faithful, loving marital bond and emotional harmony.
4. **The Outer Wrap:** The two front panels tied loosely or left flowing represent flexibility, adaptability, and the gentle resilience of rural women who balance labor with family care.
5. **Non Quai Thao (Flat Palm Hat):** Often paired with the dress, the large hat with silk tassels shields from the elements while adding a mystical, poetic elegance.

## Visual & Material Features
- **Color Palette:** Earthen colors (brown from củ nâu, deep indigo, betel-nut black) for daily labor, layered over vibrant inner colors (lotus pink yem, canary yellow or emerald green belt) for festivals and Quan Ho folk singing.
- **Layers:** Outer four-panel robe, inner white blouse, bright Yem bib, flowing black skirt (Vay linh), silk sash, and velvet headscarf (Khan mo qua) shaped like a crow's beak.

# TW_fest.md: Taiwan Lantern Festival & Pingxi Sky Lanterns

## Basic Information
- Traditional Chinese Title: 台灣平溪天燈節 / 台灣元宵燈會
- English Title: Taiwan Lantern Festival & Pingxi Sky Lantern Festival
- Origin: Pingxi District, New Taipei City, Taiwan
- Timing: 15th day of the 1st Lunar Month (Yuanxiao Festival / Lantern Festival)
- Global Recognition: Named by Discovery and National Geographic as one of the world's most spectacular cultural celebrations.

## Short Introduction
The Pingxi Sky Lantern Festival is an awe-inspiring spectacle where thousands of glowing paper lanterns carrying handwritten hopes, prayers, and blessings are released simultaneously into the dark night sky, illuminating the lush mountain valleys with golden light.

## Deep Cultural Philosophy & Symbolism
1. **Historical Origin (Signal of Safety):**
   - In the 19th century, mountainous Pingxi was frequently raided by bandits. Villagers hid in the hills. When danger passed, watchmen released sky lanterns (invented by Zhuge Liang / Kongming) to signal 'Peace and Safety' (報平安), calling villagers back home.
2. **Releasing the Old, Welcoming the New (放天燈 - Praying for Blessings):**
   - Today, releasing a lantern symbolizes letting go of past year's sorrows, worries, and sickness, and sending aspirations for health, career success, and love directly up to Heaven.
3. **The Four-Sided Color Symbolism:**
   - Red for good luck and peace (平安).
   - Yellow for wealth and prosperity (發財).
   - Blue for career success and wisdom (事業).
   - Pink for romance and happy relationships (姻緣).
4. **Collective Unity & Harmony:**
   - Watching countless lanterns rise into the starry night creates a shared spiritual bond of collective human hope, peace, and universal benevolence.

## Visual & Material Features
- **Materials:** Bamboo frame, thin rice paper/cotton paper, wire, and a central joss paper bundle soaked in kerosene.
- **Visuals:** Thousands of radiant lanterns floating like a galaxy of rising stars against the misty mountains of Pingxi.
